# DeskifyOfficeSupply-PowerBI-CaseStudy

A Business Intelligence project developed using Power BI to transform Deskify Office Supply Co.'s sales and transaction data into actionable business insights.

🔍 Project Overview

    This project analyzes over four years of sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, 
    and regional trends to support strategic decision-making.

⚠️ Problem Statement

      The organization relied heavily on Microsoft Excel to compile, analyze, and report monthly sales and transaction data.
      
      As sales volumes increased, the reporting process became:
      
      1. Time-consuming and inefficient
      2. Highly dependent on manual data entry
      3. Prone to reporting errors and inconsistencies
      4. Difficult to consolidate across multiple data sources
      5. Limited in providing real-time business insights


📖 Project Objective

    The aim of this project was to develop a Power BI reporting solution that would for Deskify Office Supply that will enable them
    centralize sales and transaction data into a single reporting environment, automate data transformation and cleaning processes,
    improve reporting accuracy and consistency, reduce reliance on manual Excel reporting, provide interactive dashboards for faster business analysis,
    and enable data-driven decision-making across departments.

🏷️ Data Dictionary

    Date: Date of the sales transaction Store: Store location
    Product: Product name or description
    Category: Product category (e.g., clothing, shoes, accessories)
    Gender: Gender of the target market for the product (e.g., male, female, unisex)
    Quantity: Number of units sold
    Sales Amount: Sales amount for each product
    Customer ID: Unique identifier for each customer (for tracking repeat purchases and customer
    behaviour)
    Discount: Discount applied to the transaction (if any)
    Payment Type: Payment method used for the transaction (e.g., cash, credit card, debit card, online
    payment)

    2.	Customer Analysis
    3.	Product Analysis
  

📌 Highlights

    10.33% Profit Margin
    5,496 Total Orders
    795 Total Customers
 

📈 Insights Generated

    Product Performance:
    * Office Supplies generated the highest profit contribution.
    * Binders, Paper, and Furnishings emerged as the most profitable product sub-categories.
    * Copiers recorded negative profitability, indicating potential pricing or cost challenges.

    Customer Insights:
    * Corporate customers generated the highest overall profit.
    * Home Office customers ranked second in profitability.
    * A small group of customers contributed significantly to overall revenue and profit.

    Regional Insights:
    * Several states consistently outperformed others in profitability.
    * Regional performance highlights opportunities for targeted expansion and localized marketing strategies.

    Operational Insights:
    * Regular Air shipping accounted for the majority of orders.
    * Monthly profitability showed seasonal fluctuations, with strong performance during September and October.


    💡 Recommendations

    Product Strategy:
    * Increase investment in top-performing product categories.
    * Review pricing and inventory strategies for underperforming products.

    Customer Strategy:
    * Strengthen relationships with high-value corporate customers.
    * Implement customer loyalty and retention programs.
    * Regional Growth
    * Expand operations in high-performing regions.

    Regional Growth:
    * Expand operations in high-performing regions.
    * Investigate factors limiting performance in lower-profit states.

    Operational Efficiency:
    * Optimize shipping and logistics costs.
    * Continue monitoring monthly sales patterns to improve forecasting accuracy.
