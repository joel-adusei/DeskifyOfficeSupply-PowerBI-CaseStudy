# DeskifyOfficeSupply-PowerBI-CaseStudy

  Deskify Office Supply Power BI Case Study
  
  A Business Intelligence project developed using Power BI to transform Deskify Office Supply Co.'s sales and transaction data into actionable business insights.

🔍 Project Overview

  This project analyzes over four years of sales performance, customer behavior, product profitability, and regional trends to support strategic decision-    making.

  The solution replaces manual Excel-based reporting with an automated and interactive Power BI dashboard, enabling stakeholders to monitor business performance efficiently and make data-driven decisions.

🏢 Business Introduction

  Deskify Office Supply Co. is a leading retailer specializing in office supplies, technology accessories, and furniture.
  
  The company serves both individual consumers and corporate clients, offering a diverse product portfolio designed to meet workplace and home-office needs.
  
  Over the years, Deskify has experienced substantial growth, resulting in increased transaction volumes and more complex reporting requirements. To maintain operational efficiency and improve decision-making, the organization required a modern business intelligence solution capable of delivering real-time insights into sales performance, customer behavior, and product profitability.

⚠️ Business Problem

  The organization relied heavily on Microsoft Excel to compile, analyze, and report monthly sales and transaction data.
  
  As sales volumes increased, the reporting process became:
  
  Time-consuming and inefficient
  Highly dependent on manual data entry
  Prone to reporting errors and inconsistencies
  Difficult to consolidate across multiple data sources
  Limited in providing real-time business insights
  
  Management lacked a centralized reporting system capable of tracking key business metrics and identifying emerging trends quickly.

🎯 Project Objective

  The objective of this project was to develop a Power BI reporting solution that would:
  
  Centralize sales and transaction data into a single reporting environment
  Automate data transformation and cleaning processes
  Improve reporting accuracy and consistency
  Reduce reliance on manual Excel reporting
  Provide interactive dashboards for faster business analysis
  Enable data-driven decision-making across departments

The final solution delivers an executive-level reporting experience with interactive visualizations and actionable insights.

📌 Key Highlights
  Financial Performance
  $14.74M Total Revenue
  $1.52M Total Profit
  10.33% Profit Margin
  5,496 Total Orders
  795 Total Customers
  
Product Performance

  * Office Supplies generated the highest profit contribution.
  * Binders, Paper, and Furnishings emerged as the most profitable product sub-categories.
  * Copiers recorded negative profitability, indicating potential pricing or cost challenges.
  * Customer Insights
  * Corporate customers generated the highest overall profit.
  * Home Office customers ranked second in profitability.
  * A small group of customers contributed significantly to overall revenue and profit.
  * Regional Insights
  * Several states consistently outperformed others in profitability.
  * Regional performance highlights opportunities for targeted expansion and localized marketing strategies.
  * Operational Insights
  * Regular Air shipping accounted for the majority of orders.
  * Monthly profitability showed seasonal fluctuations, with strong performance during September and October.

  💡 Recommendations
Product Strategy

  Increase investment in top-performing product categories.
  Review pricing and inventory strategies for underperforming products.

Customer Strategy

  Strengthen relationships with high-value corporate customers.
  Implement customer loyalty and retention programs.
  Regional Growth
  Expand operations in high-performing regions.
  Investigate factors limiting performance in lower-profit states.
  Operational Efficiency
  Optimize shipping and logistics costs.
  Continue monitoring monthly sales patterns to improve forecasting accuracy.
